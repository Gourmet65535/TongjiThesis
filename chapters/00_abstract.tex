\MakeAbstract{
    多校区办学背景下，校内出行已不再只是固定班车调度问题。师生在跨校区上课、参加活动或往返宿舍时，往往要同时比较班车、公共交通和临时拼车方案。现有信息通常分散在通知、网页和社交群中，查找成本高，拼车过程也缺少统一记录。针对这一情况，论文设计并实现了一个基于 Spring Boot 的校内综合交通平台。平台采用前后端分离架构，以微信小程序作为统一入口，后端按用户、拼车、论坛、聊天、文件和交通等业务拆分服务，并结合 MySQL 与 MongoDB 完成混合存储。

    平台实现的重点放在拼车业务。校园拼车的特点是路线较集中、时间约束明确，但用户对出发时段和同行对象的接受程度并不相同。基于这一特点，系统把匹配处理分成两步：先按起点、终点、日期、时间区间和剩余座位筛掉不满足条件的订单，再用随机森林模型对候选结果排序。模型输入包括空间匹配、时间重叠、信用分、完成率和迟到率等特征，用于区分候选订单的优先级。除拼车外，平台还提供论坛发帖与评论、私聊沟通、交通时刻表查询和行程反馈等功能，使信息发布、同行确认和结果回写能够在同一系统内完成。

    测试结果表明，平台能够完成用户登录、拼车订单处理、论坛互动、即时通信和交通信息查询等主要流程；拼车查询结果与场景约束基本一致，两类高频查询接口在给定并发条件下保持稳定响应。论文工作的落点在于把分散的校园出行信息和拼车流程收拢到一个可运行的平台中，并给出一套可实现的候选排序方法，可为高校内部出行服务建设提供工程层面的参考。
}{校内综合交通平台，微信小程序，Spring Boot，拼车匹配，随机森林}

\MakeAbstractEng{
    Under a multi-campus teaching arrangement, campus travel is no longer limited to fixed shuttle scheduling. Faculty and students often compare shuttle services, public transport, and temporary carpool options for the same trip, yet the relevant information is usually scattered across notices, web pages, and social groups. To address this problem, this paper designs and implements a campus comprehensive transportation platform based on Spring Boot. The platform adopts a front-end and back-end separation architecture, uses a WeChat Mini Program as the unified entry, and divides the back-end into user, carpool, forum, chat, file, and transport services with hybrid persistence based on MySQL and MongoDB.

    The main implementation effort is concentrated on carpool organization. Campus trips usually have clear route and time constraints, while users still differ in their tolerance for departure windows and ride partners. For this reason, the matching process is divided into two stages. Candidate orders are first filtered by origin, destination, date, time interval, and remaining seats, and a random forest model is then used to rank the feasible candidates. The model uses features such as spatial matching, time overlap, user credit, completion rate, and late rate. Besides carpooling, the platform also supports post publishing and commenting, private messaging, timetable query, and trip feedback, so that information retrieval, travel coordination, and result feedback can be completed within one system.

    Experimental results show that the platform can complete major processes including user login, carpool order handling, forum interaction, instant messaging, and transport information query. The matching results remain consistent with scenario constraints, and the high-frequency query interfaces stay stable under the tested concurrency setting. The work of this paper is aimed at a concrete campus scenario and offers an implementable solution for information integration, carpool workflow organization, and candidate ranking.
}{Campus Comprehensive Transportation Platform, WeChat Mini Program, Spring Boot, Carpool Matching, Random Forest}
