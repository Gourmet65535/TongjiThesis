\MakeAbstract{
    随着高校多校区办学模式的推进，校区间通勤、校内外交通衔接及节假日期间集中出行问题日益突出。传统校园交通信息分散，拼车组织依赖非正式社交渠道，公共交通与校内班车信息缺乏统一入口，导致师生出行决策成本高、供需匹配效率低。针对上述问题，本文设计并实现了一种基于Spring Boot的校内综合交通平台。系统采用前后端分离与微服务架构，前端以微信小程序作为统一用户入口，后端围绕用户管理、拼车服务、论坛交流、即时通信、文件管理及公共交通信息查询构建独立服务，并结合MySQL与MongoDB实现混合存储，以兼顾结构化业务数据一致性和内容型数据灵活性。

    在核心业务中，本文提出“规则筛选+随机森林排序”的二层拼车匹配机制：系统先根据出行起点、终点、日期、时间区间及剩余座位筛选候选订单，再结合空间匹配、时间重叠、用户信用、完成率和迟到率等特征，利用随机森林模型对候选结果排序，提高匹配合理性和可用性。平台同时支持帖子发布与评论、用户私聊、时刻表查询及行程反馈，实现信息获取、出行组织与用户交互的一体化服务。

    通过单元测试、集成测试和系统测试验证，平台能够稳定完成用户登录、拼车订单管理、论坛互动、即时通信及交通信息查询等核心业务流程，拼车匹配算法生成的推荐结果合理。研究与实现为高校智慧交通服务平台建设提供可行工程参考，也为校园共享出行场景下的数据整合与智能匹配研究提供实践依据。
}{校内综合交通平台，微信小程序，Spring Boot，拼车匹配，随机森林}

\MakeAbstractEng{
    With the development of multi-campus operation in universities, issues such as inter-campus commuting, integration of on-campus and off-campus transportation, and concentrated travel during holidays have become prominent. Traditional campus transportation information is scattered, carpool organization relies on informal social channels, and information on public transportation and campus shuttle buses lacks a unified access point, resulting in high decision-making costs and low supply-demand matching efficiency for faculty and students. To address these issues, this paper designs and implements a campus comprehensive transportation platform based on Spring Boot. The system adopts a front-end and back-end separation and microservice architecture, with the front-end using a WeChat Mini Program as the unified user entry, and the back-end providing independent services for user management, carpool service, forum interaction, instant messaging, file management, and public transportation information query. MySQL and MongoDB are combined for hybrid storage to balance the consistency of structured business data with the flexibility of content-oriented data.

    For core business, a two-layer carpool matching mechanism of ``rule filtering + random forest ranking'' is proposed. The system first filters candidate orders based on origin, destination, date, time interval, and remaining seats, then ranks them using a random forest model considering spatial matching, time overlap, user credit, completion rate, and late rate, improving the rationality and usability of results. The platform also supports post publishing and commenting, private messaging, timetable query, and trip feedback, providing an integrated service that combines information acquisition, travel organization, and user interaction.

    Unit, integration, and system testing show that the platform stably completes core processes including user login, carpool order management, forum interaction, instant messaging, and transportation information query, with the matching algorithm producing reasonable recommendations. The research provides a practical reference for building intelligent campus transportation platforms and offers guidance for data integration and intelligent matching in campus shared travel scenarios.
}{Campus Comprehensive Transportation Platform, WeChat Mini Program, Spring Boot, Carpool Matching, Random Forest}
